Where Broker Companies Fit

  • Operators call DLC: “We need to run title on X tracts” or “Lease up this prospect area” — then: how much time do you have?
  • Two weeks? We’ll put 20 people on it. In-house teams can’t surge like that.

What About AI?

The next five years will evolve faster than the last twenty.

09

AI & The Landman

09

AI Doesn’t Replace the Landman

  • Land work is relationship-based, judgment-based, and courthouse-based — AI doesn’t change that foundation
  • AI can assist with research, QC, and documentation — it doesn’t replace the landman negotiating with a 75-year-old rancher in Garfield County
  • The energy transition across multiple sectors is increasing demand for qualified landmen, not reducing it

The Stack

09

The Stack You Should Already Be Using

None of this is land-specific. That is the point — you can be fluent in all of it before your first day.

01

Capture Everything

Granola or any good transcription tool. Landowner calls, mentor sessions, site visits. In this business you get told something once, in a truck, that matters six months later. Stop relying on memory and a legal pad.

02

Work By Voice

Wispr Flow and tools like it. Dictate at talking speed instead of typing speed, and learn to navigate by voice. A small change that compounds every single day you work.

03

Agents On Your Machine

Claude Code, Codex. Not the chat window — these run against your actual folders. They organize files, read the whole stack, and help you decide. Give them real context and they give you real work back.

04

Build And Ship

Learn to find and use Skills other people have already written. GitHub, so you understand where work lives and never lose it. Vercel, so you can put something real into the world. Then go build one thing.
